# BrightSenApps

Building technology that makes a difference.

BrightSenApps is an independent software company focused on creating practical, accessible, and family-friendly applications that solve real-world problems.

## Our Mission

We believe technology should be simple, inclusive, and genuinely helpful. Every product we build is designed with real users at its core, guided by feedback from families, carers, educators, and professionals.

## Our Products

### Bright Speak

Bright Speak is an AAC and communication support app designed to help children express their needs, feelings, and thoughts with confidence.

Features include:

* Visual communication boards
* Speech output
* Learning activities
* Interactive mini-games
* Custom content management
* Accessibility-focused design
* No advertisements
* Privacy-first approach
